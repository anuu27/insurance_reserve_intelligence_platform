# Business Use Cases

## Executive Positioning

ActuaryTwin should be positioned as an insurance liability digital twin, not merely as a reserve predictor. The digital twin framing is stronger because it emphasizes:

- dynamic simulation
- decision support
- stress testing
- scenario planning
- optimization and capital efficiency

## Technique to Business Interpretation

- Classical reserve solver -> benchmark actuarial ground truth
- PINN reserve model -> real-time differentiable reserve engine
- Sensitivity analysis -> driver attribution for reserve movement
- Stress testing -> adverse scenario preparedness
- Optimization engine -> pricing and solvency trade-off support
- Digital twin engine -> forward-looking liability simulation

## Technique to Business Use Case Mapping

- Reserve estimation -> valuation acceleration and portfolio monitoring
- Mortality sensitivity -> underwriting and reinsurance risk review
- Interest-rate sensitivity -> asset-liability management
- Inflation stress -> benefit cost escalation analysis
- Lapse stress -> persistency and premium adequacy analysis
- Premium optimization -> profitability management
- Regime simulation -> business planning and capital allocation

## Digital Twin Positioning

The digital twin concept means a living computational representation of insurance liabilities that can be queried repeatedly under changing assumptions. In practice this means:

- forecasting reserves through time
- cloning and stressing portfolios
- testing alternate macro regimes
- tracing sensitivities immediately from the learned surrogate

## Stress Testing Use Cases

- Mortality pandemic shock assessment
- Rate-down scenario impact on liability value
- Inflation-driven benefit escalation review
- Longevity improvement stress for model robustness
- Lapse deterioration impact on premium flows

## Optimization Use Cases

- Find the rate assumption consistent with a target reserve
- Improve premium adequacy while controlling reserve pressure
- Maximize a profitability proxy under solvency constraints

## Capital Efficiency Use Cases

- Compare reserve intensity across segments
- Measure how reserve changes under risk and macro drivers
- Identify policies or cohorts with disproportionate capital strain
